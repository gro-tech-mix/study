Have Questions in your mind?
01
What services does Property Guidance LLP provide?
We offer comprehensive property consultancy services, including government approvals, property verification, statutory NOCs, land development consultancy, structural engineering, RERA compliance, and technical guidance for residential, commercial, and industrial projects.
02
Which government authorities do you assist with?
We provide liaisoning and consultancy services for approvals from BBMP, BDA, BMRDA, KIADB, BESCOM, BWSSB, Fire Department, AAI, HAL, KSPCB, RERA, and other relevant authorities based on your project requirements.
03
Why is property verification important before purchasing a property?
Property verification helps identify technical, regulatory, and planning issues that may not be covered by a legal opinion. It ensures you make an informed investment by verifying site conditions, government records, land use, road widening, buffer zones, and statutory compliance.
04
How do I get started with Property Guidance LLP?
Simply contact our team through the website, phone, or email. We'll understand your project requirements, review your documents, and recommend the most suitable consultancy services for your needs.
